Stormhorn is the northernmost settlement in the [[BlackWater]] region.

# Overview

Stormhorn is a weatherworn harbor town along the rocky shores of [[BlackWater]], where cold rain, crashing waves, and endless sea mist have shaped generations of hardened people. Built atop black stone cliffs and sprawling across rotting docks driven deep into dark waters, the town survives beneath the constant roar of the restless sea from which it takes its name.

Once a thriving fishing and trade settlement, Stormhorn has fallen into decline as the ocean has grown increasingly hostile. Nets return empty, ships vanish beyond the fog, and black tides carrying dead fish and wreckage now wash ashore. Hunger and desperation spread through the town, driving some toward smuggling, superstition, and whispered rumors of strange things lurking beneath the waves.

Yet despite its decay, Stormhorn remains stubbornly alive. Whale oil lanterns still flicker across the docks at dusk, market bells still ring through the sea spray each morning, and crowded taverns fill with sailors seeking warmth from the bitter coastal winds. But beneath the town’s weary routine lingers a growing sense of dread — the feeling that the sea is watching, waiting, and slowly drawing closer with every passing tide.
