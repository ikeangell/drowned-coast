# Overview

The Drowned Coast is a land of black seas, dying kingdoms, and ancient things best left beneath the waves. Stretching along a storm-ravaged shoreline, its fractured realms are bound together by trade, superstition, and fear of the deep.

Fog blankets the coast for much of the year, swallowing fishing villages, ruined lighthouses, and crumbling cliffside cathedrals. Sailors speak of ghost ships drifting silently through the mist, while entire settlements vanish beneath the tide without warning. Along the shore, the dead are buried with iron bells tied to their wrists, lest they rise again when the sea calls their names.

Across the Drowned Coast, whispers persist of an approaching end:  
that the sea is rising,  
that death itself has begun to fail,  
and that something ancient has started to wake beneath the waves.
