Blackwater Reach lies along the northern edge of the [[The Drowned Coast]], where jagged cliffs, restless seas, and endless mist smother the shoreline beneath cold gray skies. Once known for its thriving fisheries and bustling harbor towns, the region has fallen into slow decay as the ocean itself has turned hostile.

In recent years, dark tides have begun washing ashore — blackened waters carrying dead fish, shattered ships, and bloated corpses from the deep. Violent storms gather without warning, swallowing entire crews beneath towering waves while unnatural fog creeps inland for days at a time. Fishermen whisper that the sea no longer sleeps.

The people of Blackwater endure rather than live. Villages cling desperately to the coast atop rotting docks and waterlogged foundations. Hunger spreads through the region as fishing yields dwindle, leaving many families wondering where their next meal will come from. Some turn to smuggling, piracy, or grave robbing to survive. Others turn to prayer.

[[Stormhorn]] is the northernmost settlement in BlackWater.
