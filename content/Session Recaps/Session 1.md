Being received into the BlackWater coast, the crew floats into Stormhorn. The local dockhands seem as beaten by life as the the town does, and don't seem to notice the second ship coming into the docks as the town bell tolls 3 times. Encouraged to check out the ship ourselves, we find the ship to be good condition albeit apparently empty. After a quick surprise attack, we leave the ship with its waterlogged corpse and take a captain's log that appears to change itself. Lightning strikes the statue across the harbor as one of the dockhands calls out to a man named Thomas and launches himself into the black waters of the sea. - Einar Thorson
